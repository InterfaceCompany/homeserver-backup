Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

homeserver-backup is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses the Google Drive permission "drive.file", which lets it see only the files it creates itself.

  • It stores encrypted backup files in the owner's own Google Drive. The files are encrypted before they leave the owner's server.
  • It does not read, collect or share any other Google account data.
  • It does not send data to anyone else, and it has no analytics or advertising.
